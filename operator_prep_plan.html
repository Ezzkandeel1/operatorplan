Daily Pull-Up Rule
Every single training day — before you leave the gym: 3 sets of pull-ups to failure. No counting. Just go until you physically can't. This alone gets you to 15+ by Month 4.
Running in Phase 1
Pace: Conversational. If you can't speak in full sentences you're going too fast. This is Zone 2 — fat burning, not performance.

Surface: Treadmill or grass only until you hit 94 kg. At 102 kg, hard pavement destroys knees over time.

Distance progression: Week 1–4: 2km · Week 5–8: 3km · Week 9–16: 4–5km

Nutrition Shift in Phase 3
Calories go up to 2,800–3,000 kcal. High cardio volume needs fuel. Don't under-eat here — your body will break down.

Carbs become critical: Aim for 300g+ carbs on run days. Rice, oats, bread, fruit. Without carbs your run performance collapses.

Protein: Stay at 200g+ minimum even with higher calories.
