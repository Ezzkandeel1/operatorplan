The Daily Pull-Up Rule
MANDATORY FINISHER: Every single gym day, before you leave the facility, execute 3 sets of pull-ups to absolute physical failure. Do not count. Put in 100% effort. This volume builds structural endurance rapidly.
Running in Phase 1 (Under 94 kg Rules)
  • PACE: Conversational (Zone 2). You must be able to speak full sentences comfortably.
  • SURFACE: Treadmill or flat grass fields only. At 102 kg, hard asphalt running will destroy joint integrity.
  • PROGRESSION: Weeks 1–4: 2 km per run // Weeks 5–8: 3 km per run // Weeks 9–16: 4–5 km per run.

Peak Nutrition Strategy Shift
  • FUEL INTAKE: Daily target calories must increase to 2,800 - 3,000 kcal. Do not run on a severe caloric deficit now. Your body will enter catabolic muscle breakdown.
  • CARBOHYDRATES: Aim for 300g+ of complex carbs on heavy run and rucking days. Complex grains, oats, white rice, clean bread, and recovery fruits are mandatory.
  • PROTEIN METRICS: Maintain a minimum baseline threshold of 200g+ of pure protein.
